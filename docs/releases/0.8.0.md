# Render 0.8.0 — Magnetic storyline

- Optional magnetic mode with an explicit primary video track, automatic removal of implicit gaps, and primary clip/group reordering.
- Connected clips follow their anchors during moves, packing and leading ripple trims. Newly added secondary clips connect automatically in magnetic mode; existing clips can be connected or disconnected explicitly.
- Splitting/inserting through an anchor reconnects later attachments to the right segment. Multitrack paste links copied children to copied anchors.
- Explicit gap clips retain intentional pauses and render black. Insert a two-second gap, then trim it normally.
- Locked connected tracks block movement/deletion of their anchors. All edits remain validated, atomic and undoable.
- Traditional track mode remains available. Changing the primary track clears old connections as one undoable command; moving an anchor off the primary detaches its children, while deleting it in magnetic mode removes connected clips.
- Schema 5 stores timeline mode, connections and generated gaps; older projects migrate with traditional editing as the default.

Validation covers Debug/Release tests for bidirectional reordering, connection timing, locked-track rejection, explicit gaps, source-phase trimming, copied-anchor identity, serialization and undo snapshots, plus installed-app magnetic move/gap/undo/playback checks.

Apple Silicon, macOS 14+. Ad-hoc-signed ARM64 Release DMG; not notarized. Development prerelease. Connections currently have one level. Overlapping connected clips are rejected rather than automatically moved to new lanes; compound/multicam and advanced transition workflows remain under development.
