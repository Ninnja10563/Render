# Render 0.15.0 — Multicam sources and angle cuts

- Create a multicam source from a selected video clip and two to sixteen imported camera recordings, with explicit source synchronization offsets.
- A paged source-angle viewer plays up to four muted cameras together and follows timeline scrubbing. It marks unavailable source time clearly and supports original/proxy/optimized playback modes.
- Click a camera to cut at the playhead, or choose a camera in the inspector to replace the selected segment. Cuts preserve source offsets, speed, animation phase and undo history.
- Original media remains shared. Playback/export use the actual selected camera’s source range through the existing compositor and audio pipeline.
- Source availability, malformed references, duplicate cameras, locked tracks and timeline bounds are validated. Schema 9 stores reusable camera definitions and clip memberships.

Validation covers source offsets, speed, cuts, undo snapshots, serialization, invalid source ranges, detached audio and encoded-camera preview/export comparisons, plus native Debug/Release and installed-app checks.

Apple Silicon, macOS 14+. Ad-hoc signed, not notarized; development prerelease. Camera cuts currently pause while the sequence rebuilds. Audio follows video; fixed master audio, timecode synchronization, drift correction and uninterrupted live switching remain future work. Camera offsets are entered manually; the separate audio synchronization tool aligns ordinary timeline clips.
