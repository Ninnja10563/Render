# Render 0.21.0 — Continuous reverse preview

- J now runs continuous reverse preview when AVFoundation cannot play a composition backward natively, instead of stepping back only once.
- Repeated J increases reverse speed through 1×, 2× and 4×. K/Space pause, L returns to forward playback, and scrubbing cancels outstanding reverse seeks.
- A monotonic clock chooses the target frame. Slow decoding drops overdue frames rather than building a seek backlog. Reverse preview stops at the beginning and follows the timeline playhead.
- The viewer identifies fallback reverse preview and its muted audio. Audio meters clear during silent reverse preview.

Tests cover fractional frame rates, clock catch-up and bounds, plus native continuous reverse seeking, pause, scrub cancellation, direction changes and stopping at zero. This is a viewer transport improvement; reverse clip effects and exported speed ramps remain separate work.

Apple Silicon, macOS 14+. Ad-hoc signed, not notarized; development prerelease. No project schema change. Full-rate reverse seeking remains dependent on media, effects and available decoding performance.
