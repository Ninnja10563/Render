# Render 0.27.0 — Timeline and editing controls

A denser editing workspace and more direct access to editing commands.

- Editing layout places the inspector above the media/effects browser on the left, with a larger viewer above the timeline on the right. Viewer display controls sit below the picture. Panels remain resizable and independently hideable.
- Thin neutral playhead with a ruler handle, neutral track selection, smaller regular-weight track names and compact, aligned controls. The timeline toolbar and clip label strips take less space. Compact 48-point tracks use squared clip blocks; drag targeting shares the same lane geometry.
- Compact media thumbnails with a persistent list-view option, flat effects lists, consistent panel titles and tighter inspector sections.
- Right-click track headers or empty lanes to select clips, paste at the playhead, lock, hide, mute or solo tracks, choose the primary storyline and add tracks.
- Clip menus include Cut and Copy. Delete and Ripple Delete preserve an existing multiple-clip selection when invoked on a selected clip.
- Ruler menus provide marker navigation/deletion and snapping; viewer menus provide playback, fit, actual pixels and preview quality.
- Track buttons have larger hit regions and explicit accessibility states.

Native macOS validation includes installed-app dark/light captures, compact and wide layouts, timeline mouse dragging and actual right-click menu actions, viewport scrolling, focused inspector commits and keyboard routing, alongside core/media tests and audio DSP sanitizers. These checks do not establish physical-machine playback performance; reports of sluggish interaction still need targeted profiling.

Apple Silicon, macOS 14+. Ad-hoc signed development prerelease, not notarized. No project format changes. Automatic updates are not included in this release.
